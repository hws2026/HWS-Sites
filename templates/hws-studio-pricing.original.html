All prices in USD. Annual plans and recurring add-ons are billed upfront: 12 months for the price of 10.

Enterprise: monthly and annual terms are tailored to your agreement.

Domains & services: Domain registration and renewal are separate. Website builds, migration, copywriting, training and managed updates are included only where explicitly stated.

Website management: A desktop or laptop is required to manage your website. Public websites work on mobile. Customer editors save drafts; administrators publish changes.

Plan allowances: Storage and editor seats are shared across your organization; page limits apply per website. ¹ Page allowances remain subject to supported platform limits, including the 900 KB website-data limit, separate from media storage.

Forms & reminders: Responses go to a private inbox without email notifications. Calendar reminders require a configured email provider and enabled administrator rules; usage charges are additional.
