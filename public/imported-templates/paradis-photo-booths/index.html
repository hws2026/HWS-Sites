Frequently Asked Questions

HOW MANY PHOTOS CAN I HAVE DURING MY EVENT?

Unlimited photos! You will also be provided with an online gallery of all photos taken throughout the event. 

WILL THERE BE SOMEONE TO HELP US OPERATE THE BOOTH?

Yes, we will have someone from our team on site for the whole time the booth is hired.

HOW LONG DOES IT TAKE TO SET UP THE BOOTH?

Allow an hour for set up and an hour for pack down. 

DIMENSIONS

We need at least 10 by 10 feet and access to a single power point and level ground.

IS THE SET UP TIME INCLUDED IN THE PACKAGES?

The booth is set up an hour before the run time starts and is all included in the cost. If you book a 3 hour package, that is 1 hour for setup and 3 hours of run time.

CAN THE BOOTH BE SET UP HOURS BEFORE IT STARTS?

Yes! And it is great to have the booth set up so it looks good until it is used later in the night. We do charge an idle fee of $50 per hour for when the booth sits in idle.

CAN I POST TO MY SOCIAL MEDIA STRAIGHT FROM THE PHOTO BOOTH?

Yes, you can email and text photos straight from the booth to your phone and post to social media.

The Story of Paradise Photo Booths

In 2000, our father acquired a property in the Broadway area, and by 2014, he had transformed the attic into his visionary project: Paradise Photos. What began as a modest green screen setup soon evolved into a comprehensive enterprise. He expanded his offerings beyond customized photos with multiple backdrops to include personalized merchandise such as apparel, accessories, and drinkware. This diversification naturally led to the conceptualization of a photo booth service. Notably, our father was at the forefront of photo booth technology development, pioneering advancements before they became widely available. His efforts laid a robust foundation for what was envisioned to become Paradise 360 Photo Booths.

Tragically, in July 2020, coinciding with his elder sister's birthday, God called him home.  Since then it has become our mission to ensure that his aspirations endure. His vision continues to guide us as we proudly introduce Paradise Photo Booths, carrying forward the legacy and innovative spirit that our father initiated.
