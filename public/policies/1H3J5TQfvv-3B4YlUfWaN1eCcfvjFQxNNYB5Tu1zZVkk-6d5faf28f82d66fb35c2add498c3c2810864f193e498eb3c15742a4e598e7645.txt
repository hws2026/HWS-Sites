12. IP GEOLOCATION
Husky Solutions LLC may use an IP address to estimate an approximate geographic location.
IP-derived location may identify information such as:
* Country;
* State;
* Region;
* Metropolitan area;
* City;
* Internet service provider;
* Network or organization.
IP geolocation is not guaranteed to be precise.
Factors such as:
* VPNs;
* Proxies;
* Mobile networks;
* Corporate networks;
* Satellite connections;
* Carrier routing;
* Cloud services
may cause the location associated with an IP address to differ from the user's actual physical location.
________________

* Network history;
* Organization location;
* Authentication events;
* Other legitimate security indicators.

14. PRECISE GEOLOCATION FOR SECURITY PURPOSES
In certain circumstances involving a suspected or confirmed security incident, Husky Solutions LLC may request, collect, process, or analyze more precise geolocation information where:
* The user has granted appropriate device, browser, application, or operating-system location permission;
* The applicable Service supports precise location functionality;
* Collection is permitted by applicable law; and
* The information is reasonably relevant to protecting an account, user, organization, or Husky Solutions LLC system.
This information may include location data capable of identifying a user's or device's location with significantly greater accuracy than ordinary IP-based geolocation.
________________


15. CLOSE-PINPOINT GEOLOCATION DURING SECURITY EVENTS
During a suspected, attempted, active, or confirmed security breach, Husky Solutions LLC may use available lawful security technologies to determine or verify a device's geographic location with increased precision.
Where technically available and legally authorized, this may include:
* Device-provided location;
* Browser geolocation;
* Application geolocation;
* GPS-derived location;
* Wi-Fi-based location;
* Cellular-network-derived location;
* Network-location information;
* Other technically available location signals.
Such information may permit Husky Solutions LLC to determine a location more precisely than IP-based city or regional data, including, in some circumstances, a relatively close or street-level location.
Husky Solutions LLC will not represent ordinary IP geolocation as precise GPS location.
________________


16. WHEN PRECISE SECURITY LOCATION MAY BE USED

* Attempted security-control bypass;
* Suspicious API access;
* Fraudulent billing activity;

* Other activity reasonably suggesting account or system compromise.

17. IMPOSSIBLE-TRAVEL DETECTION
Husky Solutions LLC may compare login locations and timestamps to identify circumstances where an account appears to have accessed the Services from geographically distant locations within an unrealistic period.
For example, a login occurring from one location followed shortly afterward by access from a distant region may trigger additional security review.
An impossible-travel alert does not by itself establish that unauthorized access occurred.
VPNs, proxies, mobile networks, or routing behavior may produce false positives.
________________
