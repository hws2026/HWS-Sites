* Husky Suite

1. PURPOSE
Biometric technologies may provide convenient and secure methods of authenticating individuals.
Because biometric identifiers can be sensitive and cannot ordinarily be changed as easily as a password, Husky Solutions LLC intends to apply heightened safeguards where it directly collects or stores biometric information.
________________


2. DEFINITIONS
For purposes of this Policy, “Biometric Identifier” may include, where recognized by applicable law, measurements or representations of unique physical or behavioral characteristics used to identify or authenticate an individual.
Examples may include:
* Fingerprints
* Voiceprints
* Retina scans
* Iris scans
* Hand geometry
* Facial geometry
* Other unique biometric measurements
“Biometric Information” may include information derived from a Biometric Identifier and used to identify or authenticate an individual.
The exact legal definitions vary by jurisdiction.
________________


3. PHOTOGRAPHS ARE NOT AUTOMATICALLY BIOMETRIC INFORMATION
An ordinary:
* Photograph
* Video
* Audio recording
is not automatically treated by Husky Solutions LLC as a Biometric Identifier merely because it depicts or records an individual.
However, information generated by analyzing such material to uniquely identify or authenticate an individual may be considered biometric information under applicable law.
________________


4. DEVICE-BASED BIOMETRIC AUTHENTICATION
Husky Solutions LLC Services may support biometric authentication provided by a user's:
* Smartphone
* Tablet
* Computer
* Operating system
* Hardware security device
Examples may include technologies such as:
* Fingerprint authentication
* Facial authentication
* Device biometric unlock
* Passkey authentication using a biometric device check
________________


5. DEVICE BIOMETRICS MAY REMAIN ON THE DEVICE
Where biometric authentication is performed entirely by the user's device or operating system, Husky Solutions LLC may receive only an authentication result such as:
* Approved
* Failed
* Authenticated
* Not authenticated
In such circumstances, Husky Solutions LLC does not intend to receive or store the underlying:
* Fingerprint image
* Facial template
* Iris scan
* Biometric template
used by the device.
________________


6. EXAMPLE: FACE OR FINGERPRINT DEVICE AUTHENTICATION
If a user authenticates to a Husky Solutions Service using a device's built-in biometric system, the device may verify the fingerprint or face locally.
Husky Solutions LLC may receive a cryptographic or authentication confirmation rather than the biometric identifier itself.
________________


7. PASSKEYS
Husky Solutions LLC may support passkeys.
A passkey may be unlocked through:
* Device PIN
* Fingerprint
* Face recognition
* Another device-authentication mechanism
The biometric component may remain under the control of the user's device or platform provider.
________________

35. RETENTION PRINCIPLE
Directly collected biometric information will not be retained indefinitely.
Husky Solutions LLC will maintain a retention and destruction process appropriate to applicable law.
________________


36. DEFAULT BIOMETRIC RETENTION SCHEDULE
Unless a shorter period is required by applicable law, contract, or the specific biometric notice, Husky Solutions LLC intends to permanently destroy directly collected Biometric Identifiers and Biometric Information when:
the initial purpose for collecting or obtaining the information has been satisfied; or
no later than three years after the individual's last interaction with Husky Solutions LLC,
whichever occurs first.
Where applicable law requires earlier destruction, the earlier deadline controls.
________________


37. ONE-YEAR POST-PURPOSE LIMIT WHERE REQUIRED
Where applicable law requires biometric information to be destroyed within one year after the purpose for collection expires, Husky Solutions LLC will follow that shorter deadline.
________________


38. EMPLOYMENT TERMINATION
Where biometric information is collected for workforce or employment-related security purposes and applicable law treats the purpose as ending upon termination of the employment relationship, the applicable destruction period will begin accordingly.
________________


39. EARLIER DELETION
Husky Solutions LLC may delete biometric information sooner when:
* The user withdraws from the feature
* The organization disables the feature
* The account terminates
* The information is no longer necessary
* The biometric template is replaced
subject to legitimate legal preservation obligations.
