1. ACCOUNT SECURITY
Husky Solutions LLC may require users to create an account before accessing certain Services.
Account access may be protected through one or more authentication mechanisms.
These may include:
* Email address and password
* One-time passwords
* Multifactor authentication
* Authentication applications
* Security keys
* Device authentication
* Biometric authentication performed by a supported device
* Passkeys
* Organization authentication systems
* Other supported authentication methods
Available methods may vary by Service.
________________


2. ACCURATE ACCOUNT INFORMATION
Users must provide accurate information when creating or maintaining an account.
You may not knowingly:
* Create an account using another person's identity without authorization
* Provide fraudulent contact information
* Impersonate another person
* Impersonate an organization
* Misrepresent your authority
* Create accounts for fraudulent purposes
________________


3. UNIQUE ACCOUNT IDENTIFIERS
Accounts may be associated with identifiers such as:
* Email address
* Username
* Customer ID
* Organization ID
* Internal account ID
* Employee ID
* License ID
Husky Solutions LLC may use internal identifiers to administer accounts without publicly displaying them.
________________


4. ACCOUNT OWNERSHIP
The person or organization identified as the authorized account owner is responsible for maintaining control of the account.
For organization-managed accounts, ownership and administrative control may belong to the applicable organization rather than the individual end user.
________________


5. ORGANIZATION-MANAGED ACCOUNTS
Organizations may be permitted to create or manage accounts for:
* Employees
* Contractors
* Administrators
* Managers
* Members
* Other authorized users
An organization administrator may have authority to:
* Create users
* Disable users
* Reset certain credentials
* Assign roles
* Change permissions
* Remove access
* Review organization-related activity
* Configure authentication requirements
________________


6. USER RESPONSIBILITY
Users are responsible for taking reasonable steps to protect their accounts.
Users should:
* Use strong passwords
* Avoid password reuse
* Protect authentication codes
* Protect recovery codes
* Maintain secure devices
* Keep account email addresses current
* Use multifactor authentication where available
* Report suspicious activity promptly
________________


7. PASSWORD REQUIREMENTS
Husky Solutions LLC may establish password requirements based on current security needs.
Requirements may include:
* Minimum password length
* Prohibition of commonly compromised passwords
* Restrictions on known breached passwords
* Other reasonable security controls
Password rules may change as security practices evolve.
________________


8. PASSWORD STORAGE
Husky Solutions LLC does not intend to store ordinary user passwords in readable plain-text form.
Passwords should be protected using appropriate cryptographic techniques.
Husky Solutions LLC support personnel generally should not be able to tell a user what their existing password is.
________________


9. PASSWORD CONFIDENTIALITY
Users must not intentionally disclose passwords to unauthorized persons.
Husky Solutions LLC personnel will not ordinarily ask a user to send their complete password through:
* Email
* Support ticket
* Discord
* Chat
* Direct message
A person requesting your complete Husky Solutions LLC password may be attempting fraud or phishing.
________________


10. PASSWORD CHANGES
Users may be required to change a password if Husky Solutions LLC reasonably believes:
* The password may have been compromised
* Credentials appeared in a known data breach
* Suspicious activity occurred
* An administrator requires a reset
* Another security concern exists
________________


11. FORCED PASSWORD RESETS
Husky Solutions LLC may force a password reset following:
* Suspected account takeover
* Security incident
* Credential theft
* Administrative compromise
* Exposure of authentication information
A forced reset may invalidate existing sessions.
________________


12. TEMPORARY PASSWORDS
Certain organization-management features may permit an administrator to issue a temporary password.
Temporary passwords may:
* Expire automatically
* Require replacement at first login
* Be limited to one use
* Be subject to additional verification
________________


13. MULTIFACTOR AUTHENTICATION
Husky Solutions LLC may support or require multifactor authentication (“MFA”).
MFA may use:
* Email codes
* Authenticator applications
* Security keys
* Device-based authentication
* Passkeys
* Other supported authentication factors
________________


14. MANDATORY MFA
Husky Solutions LLC may require MFA for accounts with elevated privileges.
This may include:
* Super administrators
* Organization administrators
* Billing administrators
* Security administrators
* Developers with sensitive access
* Internal administrative accounts
________________


15. ONE-TIME PASSWORDS
Certain Services may send one-time passwords (“OTPs”) for purposes such as:
* Login
* Account recovery
* Email verification
* Sensitive account changes
* Additional security verification
OTPs may expire after a limited period.
________________


16. OTP CONFIDENTIALITY
Users should never provide a one-time security code to someone who unexpectedly requests it.
Husky Solutions LLC personnel should not ordinarily need a user to verbally provide a login OTP sent for the user's own authentication session.
________________


17. OTP EXPIRATION
A one-time password may expire after:
* A defined amount of time
* Successful use
* Issuance of a replacement code
* A security event
Expired codes cannot be relied upon for continued access.
________________


18. RATE LIMITING
Husky Solutions LLC may limit:
* Login attempts
* OTP requests
* Password resets
* Account-recovery attempts
* Verification requests
to protect against:
* Brute-force attacks
* Abuse
* Automated attacks
* Account takeover
________________


19. ACCOUNT LOCKOUT
An account may be temporarily locked following suspicious authentication activity.
Examples include:
* Repeated incorrect passwords
* Repeated OTP failures
* Credential-stuffing indicators
* Suspicious recovery attempts
* Unusual administrative access
________________


20. ACCOUNT LOCKDOWN
For significant security concerns, Husky Solutions LLC may place an account into a heightened-security or lockdown state.
During lockdown, the Company may restrict:
* Login
* Password resets
* Email changes
* Administrative changes
* Billing changes
* Data exports
* API access
until additional verification is completed.
________________

Sessions may be represented through:
* Secure cookies
* Authentication tokens
* Session identifiers
* Application credentials
________________


22. SESSION EXPIRATION

* Time limits
* Inactivity
* Password changes
* Account security changes
* Logout
* Administrative action
* Security incidents
________________


23. SESSION REVOCATION
Husky Solutions LLC may revoke active sessions when reasonably necessary to protect an account.

* Password compromise
* Suspicious login
* Account takeover
* Device theft
* Security investigation
________________


24. REMOTE LOGOUT

* A device is lost

* Account compromise is suspected

Husky Solutions LLC may collect and analyze IP addresses associated with:

29. IP GEOLOCATION
An IP address may be used to estimate an approximate geographic location.

* Country
* State
* Region
* City
* Network provider
IP location information is approximate and may be inaccurate.
________________


30. PRECISE SECURITY GEOLOCATION
Where legally permitted and appropriately authorized, Husky Solutions LLC may request or use more precise device or browser location during a security event.
This may include:
* GPS
* Browser geolocation
* Wi-Fi positioning
* Cellular positioning
* Device-location services
________________


31. CLOSE-PINPOINT SECURITY LOCATION
During a suspected, attempted, active, or confirmed account compromise, Husky Solutions LLC may use available lawful location information with substantially greater precision than ordinary IP data.
This may include close or street-level device location where technically available and authorized.
Such information may be used to help:
* Verify a login
* Identify unauthorized access
* Investigate account takeover
* Investigate fraud
* Protect an organization account
* Preserve security evidence
________________


32. LOCATION IS A SECURITY SIGNAL
Geographic information is one security factor among many.
A geographic mismatch does not automatically establish unauthorized access.
For example:
* VPNs
* Proxies
* Mobile networks
* Travel
* Carrier routing
may cause unusual location signals.
________________


33. IMPOSSIBLE TRAVEL
Husky Solutions LLC may compare account access locations and timestamps to identify apparent “impossible travel.”
For example, an account appearing in geographically distant locations within an unrealistically short period may trigger:
* Additional authentication
* Session revocation
* Security review
* Account lockdown
________________


34. VPN AND PROXY USE
The use of a VPN or proxy is not automatically prohibited.
However, traffic associated with:
* VPNs
* Proxies
* Tor
* Data centers
* Known malicious infrastructure
may trigger additional security review where other suspicious indicators exist.
________________

* MFA
* Device verification
* Organization administrator approval
* Transaction verification

40. GOVERNMENT-ISSUED IDENTIFICATION

* Ownership disputes
* Fraud investigations

* Business verification issues

41. LIMITING IDENTITY DOCUMENT COLLECTION
