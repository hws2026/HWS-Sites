7.1 IP-BASED APPROXIMATE GEOLOCATION
Husky Solutions LLC may use an IP address to estimate an approximate geographic location.
This may identify information such as:
* Country
* State
* Region
* Metropolitan area
* City
* Internet service provider
* Network or organization
IP-derived location information is generally approximate.
An IP address by itself should not be interpreted as necessarily identifying a person's exact physical address, building, or GPS location.
Location estimates can be affected by:
* VPNs
* Proxy servers
* Mobile carriers
* Corporate networks
* Cloud networks
* Satellite connections
* Internet routing
* Other technical factors
________________


7.2 PRECISE OR CLOSE-PINPOINT GEOLOCATION
In certain circumstances, Husky Solutions LLC may request, collect, process, or analyze location information that is substantially more precise than ordinary IP-based geolocation.
Depending on the device, browser, application, operating system, and permissions involved, precise location information may be derived from technologies such as:
* Device location services
* Browser geolocation
* Global Positioning System (“GPS”)
* Wi-Fi-based positioning
* Cellular-network positioning
* Application-provided location
* Other authorized location technologies
Such information may, in some circumstances, indicate a device's location at a relatively close or street-level level of precision.
Husky Solutions LLC does not represent ordinary IP geolocation as GPS or exact physical-location information.

Precise or enhanced geolocation may be requested or processed when reasonably relevant to circumstances such as:
* Suspected unauthorized login

28. LOCATION DATA RETENTION
Location information is retained according to its purpose and sensitivity.
Unless another legitimate legal, contractual, or security reason requires a different period:
Approximate IP Location
Approximate location associated with ordinary security logs may generally follow the retention period for the underlying security or authentication log.
Ordinary Precise Security Location
Precise location used solely for routine account-security verification and not associated with a suspected or confirmed security breach will generally be targeted for deletion or de-identification within approximately 90 days.
Suspected Security Incidents
Precise or close-pinpoint location associated with a suspected breach may be retained while the incident is investigated.
If the investigation determines that no significant incident occurred, the information may return to the ordinary retention schedule.
Confirmed Security Incidents
Precise location materially relevant to a confirmed security incident may be retained as security evidence generally for up to two years after the incident is closed.
Information may be retained longer where reasonably necessary because of:
* Continuing investigation
* Repeated related attacks
* Legal proceedings
* Litigation
* Regulatory investigation
* Law-enforcement preservation
* Valid legal process
* Legal claims
* Other legitimate security or legal obligations
