HUSKY SOLUTIONS LLC
DATA RETENTION & DELETION POLICY
Effective Date: October 12, 2026
Last Updated: October 12, 2026
This Data Retention & Deletion Policy (“Retention Policy”) explains how Husky Solutions LLC (“Husky Solutions,” “Husky,” “Company,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) determines how long information is retained and how information is deleted, anonymized, de-identified, archived, or otherwise disposed of when it is no longer reasonably necessary.
This Policy applies, where applicable, to information processed through:
* Husky Solutions LLC websites
* Husky Suite
* HuskyCAD
* PunchPal
* Customer portals
* Administrative systems
* Hosting services
* APIs
* Billing systems
* Support systems
* FiveM licensing and resource systems
* Security systems
* Graphics and creative services
* Other current or future Husky Solutions LLC Services
This Policy should be read together with the:
* Husky Solutions LLC Privacy Policy
* Security, Incident Response & Vulnerability Disclosure Policy
* Terms of Service
* Applicable product-specific terms
________________


1. RETENTION PRINCIPLE
Husky Solutions LLC does not intend to retain personal information indefinitely merely because we have the technical ability to do so.
We seek to retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for legitimate purposes including:
* Providing Services
* Maintaining accounts
* Processing transactions
* Protecting security
* Preventing fraud
* Providing customer support
* Maintaining software licenses
* Operating customer organizations
* Resolving disputes
* Enforcing agreements
* Maintaining business records
* Complying with applicable law
* Establishing or defending legal claims
When information is no longer reasonably necessary, Husky Solutions LLC may:
* Delete it
* Destroy it
* Anonymize it
* De-identify it
* Aggregate it
* Render it inaccessible
* Securely dispose of it
as appropriate.
________________


2. RETENTION IS BASED ON PURPOSE
Retention periods may depend on:
* Type of information
* Sensitivity of information
* Applicable product
* Customer relationship
* Account status
* Legal requirements
* Security risk
* Contractual requirements
* Whether an investigation is pending
* Whether a dispute exists
* Whether litigation is reasonably anticipated
* Technical limitations
* Backup schedules
Not every category of information is retained for the same period.
________________


3. DEFAULT RETENTION SCHEDULE
Unless a different period applies under law, contract, product-specific terms, or a legitimate business need, Husky Solutions LLC generally intends to use the following retention targets.
Information Category
	General Retention Target
	Active account information
	Duration of the active account
	Deleted account information
	Generally up to 90 days after account deletion, subject to exceptions
	Customer Content
	Duration of Service plus generally up to 30–90 days following termination
	Authentication logs
	Generally up to 24 months
	Security logs
	Generally up to 24 months
	IP address security records
	Generally up to 24 months
	Ordinary precise security-location records
	Generally up to 90 days unless connected to a security incident
	Geolocation tied to suspected or confirmed security breach
	Generally up to 2 years after incident closure
	Security incident evidence
	Generally up to 2 years after incident closure or longer if legally necessary
	Fraud investigation records
	Generally up to 5 years
	Billing and transaction records
	Generally up to 7 years
	Invoices and accounting records
	Generally up to 7 years
	Contracts and order records
	Generally up to 7 years after termination or completion
	Support communications
	Generally up to 3 years after resolution
	Privacy-rights request records
	Generally up to 5 years
	Abuse reports
	Generally up to 5 years
	DMCA and intellectual-property records
	Generally up to 7 years
	FiveM purchase/license records
	Duration of license plus generally up to 7 years
	Marketing consent and opt-out records
	As long as reasonably necessary to honor the preference
	Backup copies
	Generally overwritten or expired within approximately 90 days
	De-identified or aggregated information
	May be retained longer where it no longer reasonably identifies an individual
	These periods are general operational targets and not guarantees that every individual record will exist for exactly that amount of time.
Information may be deleted earlier where appropriate.
Information may also be retained longer where permitted or required for a legitimate legal, security, contractual, or operational purpose.

4. ACTIVE ACCOUNT INFORMATION
Information necessary to maintain an active account may generally be retained for as long as the account remains active.
This may include:
* Name
* Email address

* Organization membership
* Account role
* Preferences
* Authentication settings
* Subscription status
* Account configuration
When an account is deleted or terminated, applicable deletion procedures will begin.

5. ACCOUNT DELETION
Users may be permitted to request deletion of their account.
Account deletion may cause:
* Loss of account access
* Loss of stored Customer Content
* Loss of settings
* Loss of organization access
* Loss of subscriptions or licenses where applicable
* Removal of profile information
Deleting an account does not necessarily result in immediate deletion of every record associated with the user.
Certain information may remain temporarily or longer as described in this Policy.
________________


6. POST-DELETION PROCESSING PERIOD
Following an account deletion request, Husky Solutions LLC may maintain certain account information for a limited processing period.
This period may be used to:
* Complete deletion workflows
* Prevent accidental data loss
* Detect fraudulent deletion requests
* Restore accounts where legally permitted and requested
* Process remaining transactions
* Synchronize deletion across systems
Ordinary account information targeted for deletion will generally be removed from active production systems within approximately 90 days, unless an exception applies.
________________

Husky Solutions LLC may derive approximate location information from IP addresses.

* Country
* State
* Region
* City
* Network provider

________________


21. PRECISE GEOLOCATION
Where authorized and technically available, Husky Solutions LLC may process more precise geolocation for security purposes.
This may include location derived from:
* Device location services
* Browser geolocation
* GPS
* Wi-Fi positioning
* Cellular positioning
* Other authorized location technologies
Because precise location information is sensitive, Husky Solutions LLC intends to retain it only for as long as reasonably necessary for the applicable security purpose.
________________


22. ORDINARY PRECISE SECURITY LOCATION
Precise location used solely for routine security verification and not associated with a suspected or confirmed breach will generally be targeted for deletion or de-identification within 90 days.
A shorter period may be used where appropriate.
________________


23. SUSPECTED SECURITY BREACH GEOLOCATION
Where precise, close-pinpoint, or other enhanced geolocation is associated with a suspected security breach, Husky Solutions LLC may retain the information for the duration of the investigation.
If the investigation determines no meaningful security incident occurred, the information may be deleted or returned to the ordinary security-location retention schedule.
________________


24. CONFIRMED SECURITY BREACH GEOLOCATION
If geolocation information is relevant to a confirmed:
* Account takeover
* Unauthorized login
* Data breach
* Credential theft
* Administrative compromise
* Fraud event
* Cyberattack
* Other significant security incident
Husky Solutions LLC may retain the information as security evidence.
Such information may generally be retained for up to two years after the incident is closed.
________________


25. EXTENDED SECURITY-INCIDENT RETENTION
Security information may be retained longer than two years when reasonably necessary because of:
* Continuing investigation
* Criminal investigation
* Law-enforcement request
* Legal hold
* Litigation
* Insurance claim
* Regulatory investigation
* Repeated related attacks
* Continuing threat activity
* Enforcement of Husky Solutions LLC agreements
Once the extended need ends, the information will return to the applicable deletion process.
________________

35. COOKIE DATA

36. SESSION TOKENS
Active authentication tokens are generally retained only as long as necessary to maintain the applicable session or authorized remembered login.
Tokens may be invalidated earlier because of:
* Logout
* Password change
* Account compromise
* Security response
* Account deletion
* Administrative action
________________


37. PASSWORDS
Passwords should not be retained in readable plain text.
Password hashes or related authentication data may remain for as long as necessary to maintain the applicable account.
When a password is reset, obsolete credentials may be invalidated according to the applicable authentication architecture.
________________


38. MULTIFACTOR AUTHENTICATION INFORMATION
MFA enrollment information may be retained while the applicable authentication method remains enabled.
When MFA is removed or replaced, obsolete authentication information may be deleted or invalidated.
Certain security logs showing historical MFA activity may remain according to the security-log retention schedule.
________________


39. BACKUPS
Information deleted from active systems may continue to exist temporarily in encrypted or otherwise protected backups.
Backups may be maintained for:
* Disaster recovery
* Business continuity
* System restoration
* Security recovery
Husky Solutions LLC generally intends for ordinary backup cycles containing deleted information to expire or be overwritten within approximately 90 days.
________________


40. DELETION FROM BACKUPS
Husky Solutions LLC may not immediately modify every historical backup when an individual record is deleted.
Instead, deleted information may remain inaccessible in a backup until the backup:
* Expires
* Is overwritten
* Is securely destroyed
If a backup is restored, Husky Solutions LLC may reapply applicable deletion records where technically appropriate.
________________


41. LEGAL HOLDS
Normal deletion schedules may be suspended when information is subject to a legal hold.
A legal hold may be appropriate when:
* Litigation exists
* Litigation is reasonably anticipated
* A government investigation exists
* A subpoena applies
* A court order applies
* A preservation request applies
* A contractual dispute exists
Information subject to a valid legal hold will be preserved for as long as reasonably necessary.
________________


42. LAW-ENFORCEMENT PRESERVATION
Where legally appropriate, Husky Solutions LLC may preserve records requested by law enforcement or another governmental authority.
Preservation does not necessarily mean the information will be disclosed.
Any disclosure remains subject to applicable law and the Husky Solutions LLC Privacy Policy.
________________


43. DELETION REQUEST EXCEPTIONS
A user deletion request may not require deletion of information that Husky Solutions LLC is permitted or required to retain for purposes including:
* Completing a transaction
* Detecting security incidents
* Preventing fraud
* Maintaining legal records
* Exercising legal rights
* Defending legal claims
* Complying with applicable law
* Protecting against malicious activity
* Maintaining required financial records
* Honoring another person's rights
Where possible, retained information may be limited to what is reasonably necessary for the applicable exception.
________________


44. DE-IDENTIFIED INFORMATION
Instead of deleting information completely, Husky Solutions LLC may de-identify information where appropriate.
De-identified information should not reasonably identify or be linked to a particular individual.
Husky Solutions LLC may retain genuinely de-identified information for:
* Analytics
* Security research
* Product improvement
* Performance analysis
* Business planning
for longer periods.
________________


45. AGGREGATED INFORMATION
Aggregated statistics that do not reasonably identify individual users may be retained indefinitely.

* Total number of users
* Aggregate product usage

* General traffic trends
