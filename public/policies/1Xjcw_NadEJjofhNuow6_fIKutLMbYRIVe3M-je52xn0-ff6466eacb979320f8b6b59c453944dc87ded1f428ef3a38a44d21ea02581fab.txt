1. ACCESSIBILITY COMMITMENT
Husky Solutions LLC seeks to provide digital experiences that can be used by as many people as reasonably possible, including individuals who use assistive technologies.
We recognize that users may access our Services using technologies including:
* Screen readers
* Screen magnification
* Keyboard-only navigation
* Voice-control software
* Switch devices
* Alternative input devices
* Speech recognition
* Browser accessibility features
* Operating-system accessibility features
Accessibility is an ongoing process rather than a one-time project.
________________


2. ACCESSIBILITY STANDARD
Husky Solutions LLC generally aims to design and improve applicable public-facing digital experiences with reference to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (“WCAG”) 2.2 Level AA where reasonably practicable.
WCAG is developed by the World Wide Web Consortium (“W3C”) and provides internationally recognized guidance concerning accessible web content.
WCAG is generally organized around four principles:
1. Perceivable
2. Operable
3. Understandable
4. Robust
Use of WCAG as a development target does not constitute a representation that every page, component, application, or Service currently conforms to every WCAG 2.2 Level AA success criterion.

Husky Solutions LLC may implement focus-management practices intended to assist users navigating with:

10. ERROR MESSAGES

11. COLOR AND CONTRAST

* Providing an alternative method of completing an action

43. NO MEDICAL DOCUMENTATION REQUIRED FOR ORDINARY REPORTS
Husky Solutions LLC generally does not require medical documentation merely to report an accessibility issue or request basic digital-accessibility assistance.

________________


44. RESPONSE TO ACCESSIBILITY REPORTS
When Husky Solutions LLC receives a meaningful accessibility report, we may:
1. Review the reported barrier;
2. Attempt to reproduce the issue;
3. Determine the affected Service;
4. Evaluate severity;
5. Identify a temporary workaround where available;
6. Evaluate a permanent correction;
7. Provide an appropriate response.
Some problems may require coordination with a third-party service provider.
________________


45. PRIORITIZATION
Accessibility issues may be prioritized based on factors including:
* Severity of the barrier
* Importance of the affected functionality
* Number of affected users
* Availability of alternatives
* Security requirements
* Technical complexity
* Whether the affected feature is actively supported
Barriers preventing access to essential functionality may receive higher priority.
________________


46. ACCESSIBILITY DOES NOT REQUIRE FUNDAMENTAL PRODUCT ALTERATION
Husky Solutions LLC will evaluate accessibility improvements and accommodations in light of applicable law and technical feasibility.
Nothing in this Policy should be interpreted as voluntarily requiring a modification that would:
* Fundamentally alter a Service;
* Create an unreasonable security risk; or
* Be technically impossible.
This provision does not limit obligations imposed by applicable law.
