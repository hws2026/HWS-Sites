1. WHAT ARE COOKIES?
Cookies are small text files or pieces of data stored on your browser or device when you visit a website or use an online service.
Cookies may allow a website to:
* Recognize your browser
* Maintain a login session
* Remember preferences
* Protect accounts
* Prevent fraud
* Maintain shopping or checkout sessions
* Measure website usage
* Improve performance
* Provide personalized functionality
Some cookies remain only until you close your browser, while others may remain for a longer period.
