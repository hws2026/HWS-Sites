HUSKY SOLUTIONS LLC
GOVERNMENT, LAW ENFORCEMENT & LEGAL REQUESTS POLICY
Effective Date: October 12, 2026
Last Updated: October 12, 2026
This Government, Law Enforcement & Legal Requests Policy (“Legal Requests Policy”) explains how Husky Solutions LLC (“Husky Solutions,” “Husky,” “Company,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles governmental requests, law-enforcement requests, subpoenas, warrants, court orders, preservation requests, emergency disclosure requests, civil legal process, and other legally authorized requests for information.
Husky Solutions LLC respects both:
1. The privacy and security of users; and
2. Valid legal obligations imposed upon the Company.
Husky Solutions LLC does not provide unrestricted access to customer information merely because an individual identifies themselves as a government or law-enforcement official.
Requests for non-public customer information must have an appropriate legal basis.
This Policy may apply to information associated with:
* Husky Solutions LLC websites
* Husky Suite
* HuskyCAD
* PunchPal
* Customer accounts
* Organization accounts
* Hosting services
* Customer websites
* APIs
* Billing systems
* FiveM licensing systems
* Security systems
* Customer-support systems
* Other current or future Husky Solutions LLC Services
________________


1. GENERAL POLICY
Husky Solutions LLC evaluates governmental and legal requests for customer information according to:
* Applicable law;
* Nature of the requested information;
* Jurisdiction;
* Legal authority cited;
* Scope of the request;
* Validity of the legal process;
* Privacy interests of affected users;
* Security considerations;
* Technical availability of the requested information.
Husky Solutions LLC may require clarification, correction, additional process, or narrowing where appropriate.
________________


2. NO AUTOMATIC GOVERNMENT ACCESS
Government agencies and law-enforcement agencies do not receive automatic or unrestricted access to Husky Solutions LLC systems merely because they are government entities.
Husky Solutions LLC does not provide a standing governmental “backdoor” into ordinary customer accounts.
Requests for non-public information must generally be made through legally appropriate channels.
________________


3. VALID LEGAL PROCESS
Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, Husky Solutions LLC may require legal process such as:
* Subpoena;
* Search warrant;
* Court order;
* Statutory demand;
* Administrative demand;
* Other legally enforceable process.
Different categories of information may require different forms of legal authority.
Husky Solutions LLC may require more protective legal process for information where applicable law requires it.
________________


4. REQUESTS MUST IDENTIFY LEGAL AUTHORITY
A request should identify the legal authority upon which it relies.
Where applicable, a request should include:
* Statutory authority;
* Court jurisdiction;
* Case or investigation number;
* Applicable warrant, order, or subpoena;
* Identity of requesting agency;
* Contact information for the requesting official.
________________


5. REQUESTING AGENCY INFORMATION
Government and law-enforcement requests should contain sufficient information to verify the requesting authority.
This should ordinarily include:
* Full agency name;
* Requesting officer, agent, investigator, attorney, or official;
* Official title or rank where applicable;
* Government-issued email address;
* Official telephone number;
* Agency mailing address;
* Case or investigation number;
* Supervisory or prosecutorial contact where appropriate.
Husky Solutions LLC may independently verify this information.
________________


6. REQUEST SPECIFICITY
Requests should specifically identify the account or information sought.
Useful identifiers may include:
* Account email address;
* Username;
* Customer ID;
* Organization ID;
* Domain name;
* IP address;
* Transaction ID;
* License ID;
* User ID;
* Relevant date range.
Husky Solutions LLC may reject or seek clarification of requests that do not reasonably identify the information sought.
________________


7. REQUESTS SHOULD BE NARROWLY TAILORED
Governmental requests should be appropriately limited to information relevant to the lawful purpose of the request.
Husky Solutions LLC may seek narrowing of requests that appear:
* Excessively broad;
* Unduly burdensome;
* Unrelated to the stated investigation;
* Legally defective;
* Insufficiently specific.
________________

* Device location services;
* Browser geolocation;

* Wi-Fi positioning;
* Cellular-network positioning;
* Application location services;
* Other authorized location technologies.

* Another lawful disclosure basis applies.

20. SEARCH WARRANTS
Husky Solutions LLC may respond to a legally valid search warrant issued by a court with appropriate jurisdiction.
A warrant should:
* Identify the issuing court;
* Identify the relevant legal authority;
* Describe the information sought with sufficient particularity;
* Identify the affected account or property;
* Be properly issued and executed.
________________


21. SUBPOENAS
Husky Solutions LLC may respond to valid subpoenas as required by applicable law.
A subpoena does not necessarily authorize disclosure of every type of information maintained by Husky Solutions LLC.
If the subpoena seeks information requiring a different form of legal process, Husky Solutions LLC may require the requesting party to obtain that process.
________________


22. COURT ORDERS
Husky Solutions LLC may comply with valid court orders issued by courts with appropriate authority.
The Company may seek clarification or modification of a court order where necessary.
________________


23. CIVIL SUBPOENAS
Private parties involved in civil litigation may seek information from Husky Solutions LLC through legal process.
Civil litigants do not receive unrestricted access to customer information.
Husky Solutions LLC may:
* Review jurisdiction;
* Review service;
* Evaluate privacy interests;
* Seek narrowing;
* Object to legally deficient requests;
* Require appropriate court process.

24. USER CONSENT
Husky Solutions LLC may disclose information where a user has provided legally valid consent to disclosure.
We may verify:
* Identity;
* Scope of consent;
* Authenticity of authorization.

25. PRESERVATION REQUESTS
Governmental entities may request preservation of records pending issuance of appropriate legal process where applicable law provides for such preservation.
Husky Solutions LLC may preserve relevant records without disclosing them.
Preservation and disclosure are separate actions.
________________


26. FEDERAL PRESERVATION REQUESTS
Where 18 U.S.C. § 2703(f) applies, Husky Solutions LLC will preserve covered records and evidence in its possession as required by law.
An applicable preservation request may require records to be preserved for 90 days.
A qualifying renewed request may extend the preservation period for an additional 90 days.
________________


27. PRESERVATION DOES NOT EQUAL DISCLOSURE
A preservation request does not automatically authorize Husky Solutions LLC to provide the preserved information to the requesting agency.
Appropriate legal authority may still be required before disclosure.
________________


28. PRESERVATION REQUEST CONTENT
A preservation request should identify:
* Agency;
* Requesting official;
* Investigation;
* Account or identifier;
* Categories of information to preserve;
* Applicable date range;
* Statutory authority;
* Contact information.

29. EMERGENCY REQUESTS
Husky Solutions LLC recognizes that emergencies may occur where delay could create a serious risk to human life or physical safety.
Emergency disclosure requests will be evaluated individually.
________________


30. EMERGENCY DISCLOSURE STANDARD
Where applicable law permits, Husky Solutions LLC may voluntarily disclose relevant information to a governmental entity when Husky Solutions LLC, in good faith, believes that an emergency involving danger of death or serious physical injury to a person requires disclosure without delay.
An emergency request does not guarantee disclosure.
Husky Solutions LLC determines whether an applicable emergency disclosure standard has been satisfied.
________________


31. INFORMATION THAT MAY BE RELEVANT DURING AN EMERGENCY
Depending on the circumstances and information available, an emergency disclosure may potentially include information reasonably related to addressing the emergency, such as:
* Account identifiers;
* Contact information;
* IP addresses;
* Login records;
* Approximate location;
* Precise geolocation where lawfully available;
* Relevant security information;
* Other information legally permitted to be disclosed.
Husky Solutions LLC will seek to limit an emergency disclosure to information reasonably related to the emergency.
________________


32. EMERGENCY GEOLOCATION

* Requesting official;
* Official contact information;
* Nature of the emergency;
* Person at risk;
* Description of the danger;

* Verifying an official government email domain;
* Requesting additional documentation;
* Confirming agency identity through another reliable method.
Urgency does not automatically eliminate reasonable authentication measures.
