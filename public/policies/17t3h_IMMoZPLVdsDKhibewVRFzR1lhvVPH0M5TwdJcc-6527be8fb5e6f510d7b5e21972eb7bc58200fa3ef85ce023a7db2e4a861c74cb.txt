2. DEFINITIONS
For purposes of this DPA:
“Applicable Data Protection Law”
means privacy, data-protection, or data-security law applicable to the relevant processing under this DPA.
This may include, where applicable:
* United States state privacy laws
* Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (“GDPR”)
* United Kingdom GDPR
* UK Data Protection Act 2018
* Other applicable privacy legislation
“Controller”
means a person or entity that determines the purposes and means of processing Personal Data or otherwise has a substantially equivalent role under applicable law.
“Customer Personal Data”
means Personal Data processed by Husky Solutions LLC on behalf of Customer through the Services.
“Data Subject”
means an identified or identifiable individual whose Personal Data is processed.
“Personal Data”
means information defined as personal data, personal information, or a substantially similar term under Applicable Data Protection Law.
“Process” or “Processing”
means an operation performed on Personal Data, whether automated or otherwise, including:
* Collection
* Recording
* Organization
* Storage
* Retrieval
* Consultation
* Use
* Transmission
* Disclosure
* Restriction
* Deletion
* Destruction
“Processor”
means a person or entity that processes Personal Data on behalf of a Controller or equivalent entity.
“Security Incident”
means a breach of security leading to accidental or unlawful:
* Destruction
* Loss
* Alteration
* Unauthorized disclosure of
* Unauthorized access to
Customer Personal Data where applicable law recognizes such an incident.
“Subprocessor”
means a third party appointed by Husky Solutions LLC to process Customer Personal Data on behalf of Customer.
