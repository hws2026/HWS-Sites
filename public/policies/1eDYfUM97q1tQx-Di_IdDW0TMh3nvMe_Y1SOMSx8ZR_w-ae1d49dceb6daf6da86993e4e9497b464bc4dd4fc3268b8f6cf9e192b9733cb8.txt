10. SUPPORT PRIORITY LEVELS
Husky Solutions LLC may categorize support requests according to severity.
Priority classifications help determine the order in which issues are reviewed.
________________


11. PRIORITY 1 — CRITICAL
A Priority 1 (“P1”) issue generally involves a severe production problem affecting core Service availability.
Examples may include:
* Broad Service outage
* Widespread authentication failure
* Significant production infrastructure failure
* Confirmed active security incident affecting multiple customers
* Severe data-integrity issue
P1 does not automatically apply to a problem affecting only a customer's unsupported configuration.
________________


12. PRIORITY 2 — HIGH
A Priority 2 (“P2”) issue may involve:
* Major functionality unavailable
* Significant degradation
* Important customer workflow blocked
* Serious account-access problem
* Production problem without a reasonable workaround
________________


13. PRIORITY 3 — NORMAL
A Priority 3 (“P3”) issue may include:
* Ordinary technical problems
* Configuration assistance
* Product questions
* Non-critical bugs
* Documentation questions
* General account assistance
________________


14. PRIORITY 4 — LOW
A Priority 4 (“P4”) issue may include:
* Feature requests
* Cosmetic problems
* General feedback
* Minor documentation issues
* Non-urgent questions
________________


15. PRIORITY MAY BE RECLASSIFIED
Husky Solutions LLC may change a ticket's priority based on:
* Actual technical impact
* Number of affected users
* Security risk
* Availability of a workaround
* Supported configuration
* Service scope
A customer labeling a ticket “urgent” does not automatically make the issue P1.

17. RESPONSE TIME VS. RESOLUTION TIME
“Response time” means the period before Husky Solutions LLC initially reviews or responds to a support request.
“Resolution time” means the time required to fully correct or otherwise resolve an issue.
An initial response does not guarantee immediate resolution.
Resolution may depend on:
* Technical complexity
* Customer cooperation
* Third-party providers
* Product architecture
* Software updates
* Reproduction of the issue
* Security considerations
________________
